# Kyoto sample itinerary
This fictional itinerary demonstrates document retrieval; it is not a booking or current destination guidance.
Day 1 in Kyoto: the sample plan starts with a morning walk in Higashiyama and an afternoon near the Kamo River.
Day 2 in Kyoto: the sample plan reserves the morning for Arashiyama and the afternoon for a cooking class.
For rainy weather in Kyoto, the sample plan substitutes an indoor museum visit; verify opening hours directly with the venue.
The sample budget is 15,000 JPY per day excluding accommodation and transport to Kyoto. This is an illustrative planning assumption, not a live price.
