# Sample packing checklist
This sample checklist is for demonstration and must be adapted to the actual trip.
For rain, pack a compact umbrella, a waterproof jacket, and a dry bag for electronics.
For a walking itinerary, pack comfortable shoes, refillable water, and a portable battery.
Keep a copy of your passport and reservation confirmations in a separate secure location.
Entry requirements and visa eligibility depend on nationality and itinerary; consult the official destination government website before travel.
