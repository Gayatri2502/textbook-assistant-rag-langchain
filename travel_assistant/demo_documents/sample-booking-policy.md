# Fictional booking policy
This fictional policy is a demo document, not a real hotel reservation or provider policy.
The example Sakura stay has check-in at 15:00 and check-out at 11:00.
The fictional cancellation policy allows cancellation until seven days before arrival; after that the example charge is one night.
The sample reservation includes breakfast and does not include airport transfers.
For changes to the fictional reservation, contact the sample booking agent rather than assuming availability.
